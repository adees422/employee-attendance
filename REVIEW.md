# REVIEW.md

Comprehensive review of the starter `app/main.py` implementation, covering defect identification, reproduction conditions, and resolutions.

| # | Where (function / line) | What is wrong | How you'd notice it (test, input, or symptom) | How you fixed it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `compute_late_minutes` (lines 36-37) | Off-by-one grace comparison and premature integer division truncation. | Shift `09:30`, punch-in at `09:40:01` (601 seconds). `int(601/60) = 10`. `10 > 10` evaluates to `False`, returning `0` late minutes instead of `10` (violates R2). | Checked raw second difference `diff_seconds > 600` (strictly > 10:00), then calculated `int(diff_seconds // 60)` since shift start. |
| 2 | `compute_late_minutes` (lines 33-35) | Timezone mismatch and naive datetime replacement. | Punch-in instant stored in UTC; replacing hour/minute with IST shift start creates mixed timezones off by 5h30m. | Converted punch instant to IST timezone (`UTC+05:30`), anchored `start_dt` in IST, and computed delta accurately. |
| 3 | `compute_work_hours` (line 41) | Python builtin `round()` performs Banker's Rounding (round-to-even) instead of Round Half-Up. | Work duration of 2.555 or 2.675 hours rounds down to 2.55 and 2.67 instead of rounding half-up to 2.56 and 2.68 (violates R4/R8). | Replaced with `decimal.Decimal` and `ROUND_HALF_UP` quantization to two decimal places. |
| 4 | `compute_overtime` (line 47) | Missing 30-minute threshold check. | Punching out 15 minutes after shift end returned 15 overtime minutes instead of 0 (violates R3 minimum threshold rule). | Evaluated `mins = int(diff // 60)`; returned `mins if mins >= 30 else 0`. |
| 5 | `compute_overtime` (lines 45-47) | Overnight shifts (`shift_end <= shift_start`) calculated against wrong calendar date. | Shift `22:00` - `06:00` punching out at `06:40` on July 7 compared against `2026-07-06 06:00`, yielding massive wrong overtime or 0. | Added 1 calendar day to shift end instant whenever `shift_end <= shift_start`. |
| 6 | `GET /health` (lines 72-74) | Unconditional readiness probe without verifying database connectivity. | Endpoint returns HTTP 200 even when MongoDB is down or network connection is dropped (violates contract requiring 503). | Executed `db.command("ping")` within try/except, returning HTTP 503 if unreachable. |

### Examined and confirmed NOT to be defects
1. **`emp_code` stored as string**:
   Customer business codes (e.g., `EMP0001`) are alphanumeric identifiers assigned by customer HR, distinct from MongoDB's internal `_id`. Keeping it as a string is the intended design.
2. **Dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`) and shift times (`HH:MM`) stored as plain ISO strings**:
   Per DATA_MODEL.md, calendar dates and shift times are intentionally strings to avoid accidental timezone coercion when querying across database and client boundaries.
